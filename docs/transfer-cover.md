# Portada de transferencia de archivos

Asset integrado: `public/transfer-cover-v2.png`. Imagen original creada con
generación integrada de imágenes, no CLI. Sustituye la ilustración SVG en la
tarjeta pública del login. La versión SVG anterior se conserva como alternativa.

Estilo: nube de cristal, documentos flotantes y enlaces, con iluminación azul,
cian y violeta. El título, botón y límite de 50 GB permanecen en HTML, fuera de
la imagen. La portada se sirve sin sesión para que cargue en el login.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium wide cover image for a file-transfer card on a dark website, visually paired with a polished violet/cyan 3D music cover. Primary request: make sharing files instantly recognizable and beautiful, not a generic flat folder icon. Subject: a sculptural frosted-glass cloud with an elegant upward upload arrow, three floating document cards (a cyan document, a lavender image thumbnail, a darker blue archive), and a small glossy interlocking chain-link symbol, arranged as one balanced premium composition. Backdrop: deep charcoal and midnight blue studio space with soft cyan and violet atmospheric light, very subtle flowing light trails suggesting files moving between devices. Materials: beveled edges, tactile frosted glass and satin metallic surfaces, realistic gentle reflections and soft shadows; sophisticated and restrained, not cartoon clip art. Composition: wide landscape banner approximately 2.25:1, subjects centered and fully within a generous safe area, clear recognizable silhouettes at small card size, clean darker top-left area for an HTML badge. Colors: cyan, electric blue and subtle violet, harmonious with a dark dashboard. Constraints: no words, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, people, webpage UI, or tiny decorative symbols; no oversaturated glowing clutter. The badge and copy are added separately in HTML.
